# BOSS GEAR — equipment system (user, Nov 17). IN THE GAME since Nov 17 (inventory = I); see HANDOFF.md "Boss gear + the inventory"

User picks for the build: gear saved in the browser, ONE loadout for whichever survivor you play, easy specials now (Meat Hook, Brood Mother's Sac, Antenna Headset later), enhancing included.

Inspired by MapleStory's equipment window, simpler. Progress board page: "Boss gear (planned)" tab (#equipment) on the Survival Ascension Progress artifact.

## Rules (user)
- Each boss has its own tiered gear, tiers 1-10; higher tier = rarer.
- Not everyone gets something (just luck). Defence pieces let squishy survivors get tankier.
- Gear is KEPT FOREVER (saved per player). Later: trading between players, and enhancing equipment.
- Gear does NOT change how a character looks; an equipment key (I) shows what you're wearing.
- (Nov 17, user) A boss kill bursts into 10-12 items on the ground, first come first served (they fade after ~30s).
- (Nov 17, user) Every burst has exactly 1 LEGENDARY, but not always the same one: each boss has 3 legendary variants, picked at random. Each boss also has more epic variants.

## Drop numbers (current)
- 15 items per boss (90 in all): 3 legendary (T10), 5 epic (T8-T9), 7 lower (T1-T7, one per tier).
- Every burst = 1 legendary + 1-2 epics + the rest (7-10 items): a mix of the lower 7 gear pieces and materials.
- Legendary: one of 3, each equally likely -> each variant is in 1 kill in 3 (33%). Seeing all 3 takes about 5.5 kills on average.
- Epics: 1 or 2 (50/50), each picked from the 5 -> a given epic is in about 28% of kills.
- (Nov 17, user) The other 7-10 drops are ALSO materials: gold and ores. Split (Claude's pick, easy to change): each roll is half gear, half material -> about 4 materials per burst.
- Lower gear: each roll's tier, each tier 3/4 as likely as the one below: T1 14%, T2 11%, T3 8.1%, T4 6.1%, T5 4.6%, T6 3.4%, T7 2.6% (the other half are materials).
- Chance a given lower item is in the burst: T1 73%, T2 62%, T3 51%, T4 41%, T5 33%, T6 26%, T7 20%.
- (Replaced: one item per tier T1-T10 rolled freely with at most 1 legendary and 2 epics per pile, ~20% of kills had a legendary; before that, each player rolling 50% for one item.)
- Materials (stack in a pouch, no gear slot):
  - Gold (stack 25-75 coins): Money. Every enhance costs gold; later, trading too. In 85% of kills.
  - Iron Ore (stack 1-3): Enhances gear from +1 to +4. In 75% of kills.
  - Silver Ore (stack 1-2): Enhances gear from +5 to +7. In 53% of kills.
  - Titanium Ore (stack 1): Enhances gear from +8 to +10. In 32% of kills.
  - Boss Essence (stack 1): Each boss has its own. Needed to enhance that boss's epics and legendaries. In 16% of kills.
- Boss essences: Butcher = Butcher's Bone, Alpha Hivemind = Jade Resin, Screamer = Frozen Echo, Pyre Priest = Ember Core, Mine Beast = Rune Shard, Abomination = Slag Core.
- Rarity bands: Common T1-3, Uncommon T4-5, Rare T6-7, Epic T8-9, Legendary T10 (every legendary has a special effect).

## Other ideas (first pass)
- 6 slots: Head, Outfit, Gloves, Boots, Gun Mod (upgrades your one unique gun), Trophy (a boss trinket, often a special effect).
- Stats: max HP, armour (every hit does less), damage, fire rate, move speed, skill recharge, + specials.
- With 4 players everyone usually gets a few items, but the legendary goes to whoever grabs it first.
- Duplicates stay in the bag (trading / enhancing material). Enhancing idea: +1 to +10 with ores and gold (boss essence for epics and legendaries), high + can fail.
- Saving: first version could save in the browser; the proper version needs logins + a database.

## Item hover card (Nov 17; user showed MapleStory's item tooltip as an example only, not to copy)
Shown when you hover an item in your bag or on the ground. Mock-ups on the gear tab (Rubber Boots +0, Blood-Soaked Hood +4, The Butcher's Apron +7).
- Top: rarity + tier tag, and 10 enhance pips that fill as you enhance.
- Name in the rarity colour with the + level after it; then slot · attack/defence/utility · which boss it's from.
- Slot icon (simple code-drawn line icon per slot, rarity colour) beside a compare list vs what you wear in that slot: green ▲ gains, red ▼ losses ("your slot is empty" if nothing).
- Stats: big total, with small grey base + green enhance bonus under it.
- Special box (gold) for items with a special effect.
- Next enhance: +N → +N+1, chance, ore + gold (+ boss essence).
- Footer: kept forever · tradeable later.

## Enhancing numbers (Claude's picks, easy to change)
- Each + adds 5% of the item's base stats (rounded; +10 = +50%). Special effects don't change.
- A fail uses up the materials but the item never loses a level and never breaks.
- Cost of the next level:
  - +1: 100% chance · 1 Iron Ore · 50 Gold
  - +2: 95% chance · 2 Iron Ore · 100 Gold
  - +3: 90% chance · 3 Iron Ore · 150 Gold
  - +4: 85% chance · 4 Iron Ore · 200 Gold
  - +5: 70% chance · 1 Silver Ore · 250 Gold
  - +6: 60% chance · 2 Silver Ore · 300 Gold
  - +7: 50% chance · 3 Silver Ore · 350 Gold
  - +8: 40% chance · 1 Titanium Ore · 400 Gold · +1 boss essence (epics/legendaries)
  - +9: 30% chance · 2 Titanium Ore · 450 Gold · +1 boss essence (epics/legendaries)
  - +10: 20% chance · 3 Titanium Ore · 500 Gold · +1 boss essence (epics/legendaries)
