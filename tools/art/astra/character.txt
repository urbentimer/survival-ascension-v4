 1. BASICS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Name:                          Astra
Class / role:                  Emergency Commander / Support Summoner
One-line description:          Calls police to fight, doctors to heal, and builders to create a safe enclosure.
Look (for the artist):         Adult male, medium tan skin, short swept silver-white hair with dark roots,
                               clean-shaven face, focused expression. Navy short split-hem command coat,
                               white shoulder panels, cyan collar and sleeve bands, gold star chest badge.
                               Black gloves and boots, navy trousers, headset, radio and belt-mounted tablet.
                               In sprites: chunky chibi body, head about one third of total height.
Bright accent colour:          Cyan collar and cuffs, broad white shoulders, gold chest star.
Personality / vibe:            Calm, dependable field leader; always has backup on the way.
Playstyle in one line:         Fight with a rifle, protect the team, and deploy the right support at the right time.

 4. E SKILL   (normal skill)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Name:                          Emergency Medical Team
What happens, step by step:    1) Press E. Astra makes a 0.45-second radio call; deploy exactly THREE AI doctors
                                  on free ground close to him. Their 20-second lifetime starts on deployment.
                               2) Each doctor chooses the nearest reachable injured, living playable TEAMMATE
                                  not already assigned a doctor. Measure path distance, not straight-line
                                  distance through obstacles. Resolve ties by stable player ID.
                               3) Doctors run to their patients. Within 70px and clear line of sight, each
                                  doctor channels exactly 25 HP PER SECOND, capped at the patient's maximum HP.
                                  The doctor follows a moving patient and pauses healing if range/LOS is lost.
                               4) Retarget when a patient is full, downed, dead, disconnected or unreachable.
                                  Prefer other teammates. If no injured teammate is available, one doctor may
                                  heal Astra; others follow nearby until another patient needs help.
Damage:                        None. Doctors do not attack.
Range / area:                  Select patients anywhere reachable in the active battlefield; healing range 70px.
Aim:                           No mouse aim. Automatic friendly target selection.
Cooldown:                      30 seconds from successful deployment. Recast blocked while this team is active.
Lasts:                         20 seconds, or until each doctor is defeated. Each has 100 HP and speed 205.
Status effects:                Heal 25 HP/s per patient. No overheal, resurrection, status cleanse or tower repair.
                               Multiple Astra doctors NEVER stack on one patient: maximum 25 HP/s from doctors.
                               Three doctors can provide 75 HP/s total only when healing three different players.
                               Other existing healing abilities retain their normal game rules.
Needs a specific weapon?       No.
Animation sheet(s):            sprites/astra-radio-call.png, sprites/doctor.png,
                               effects/deploy-beacon.png, effects/healing-pulse.png, skill-cards/card-E.png.
Sound (optional):              Radio chirp, short medical dispatch confirmation, soft healing pulse.

 5. Q ULTIMATE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Name:                          Police Reinforcements
Unlocks at:                    100 credited non-boss zombie kills, following the supplied kit's ultimate rule.
What happens, step by step:    1) Press Q. After a 0.45-second radio call, deploy exactly SEVEN AI police officers
                                  in separate free positions near Astra. No vehicles are required.
                               2) Officers follow in a loose formation and shoot hostile zombies and bosses.
                                  Prioritise enemies threatening nearby playable characters, then the nearest
                                  visible hostile. Officers never attack players, teammates, doctors or workers.
                               3) Each officer moves around obstacles, seeks line of sight and fires controlled
                                  bursts. If a target leaves range or dies, choose another. Friendly fire is off.
                               4) Regroup when no enemy is visible; do not chase beyond the squad leash. They
                                  retreat/despawn when their lifetime ends, or disappear when their HP reaches 0.
Damage:                        Base 10 per bullet, 3 bullets per burst, 0.12s between bullets, one burst every 1.5s.
                               Base sustained maximum: 20 DPS per officer; 140 DPS for all seven before misses.
                               Police HP: 110 each. Speed: 195. No ammunition or loot pickups.
Range / area:                  Fire range 440px; formation 70-160px from Astra; chase leash 600px from Astra.
                               Prioritise threats within 250px of any teammate inside that leash.
Lasts:                         30 seconds from deployment, or until all seven officers are defeated.
Cooldown after it ends:        60 seconds after the final officer is removed. No overlapping squads per Astra.
Animation sheet(s):            sprites/astra-radio-call.png, sprites/police-officer.png,
                               effects/deploy-beacon.png, skill-cards/card-Q.png.
Sound (optional):              Radio dispatch, brief siren chirp on arrival, standard rifle reports.

 6. STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Good against:                  Hordes, sustained team damage, dangerous pushes that can be blocked by walls.

Teamwork:                      Seven allied shooters cover the team; three doctors treat separate teammates;
                               the R enclosure gives the squad a shared defensive position.
                               Doctors and police can operate inside the enclosure and pass through its walls.

--- R ABILITY (third active skill; added without changing the supplied template headings) ---
Name:                          Safehouse Construction
Key:                           R. Available from match start; independent of E and Q.
What happens, step by step:    1) Validate an empty build footprint centred on Astra's position at key press.
                                  Astra makes the shared 0.45-second radio call. Revalidate at call completion.
                                  Spawn exactly TWO construction workers. The selected centre stays fixed.
                               2) Each worker assembles TWO walls: worker A builds north/east; worker B builds
                                  south/west. Both work simultaneously. Total construction time: 2 seconds.
                                  Scaffold frames are visual only. All FOUR walls become solid together at completion.
                               3) Four huge reinforced wall sides form a completely enclosed square. Interior is
                                  320 x 320px, walls are 24px thick, exterior footprint is 368 x 368px.
                                  North/south collision rectangles: 368 x 24px. East/west: 24 x 320px.
                                  Corner edges touch with no enemy-sized gaps. Wall artwork may be tiled along sides.
                               4) The completed walls stay for EXACTLY 45 seconds. They are indestructible during
                                  that interval and block hostile movement, melee reach, bullets, venom, fireballs
                                  and direct line-based laser attacks. Boss charges collide with the wall exterior.
                                  Bosses may not tunnel through, teleport into or spawn inside the enclosure.
                               5) Astra, all teammates and friendly summons may pass through the walls using
                                  team-filtered collision. Friendly shots pass outward. This prevents trapping
                                  teammates, and lets the doctors continue reaching patients.
                               6) The two workers leave after building. At 5 seconds remaining the wall outline
                                  flashes; at expiry all four collision sides disappear together, then a brief
                                  harmless dismantle animation plays. No damaging collapse or debris.
Damage:                        None. No crush damage; no construction-worker attacks or healing.
Protection limits:             This is a barrier, not a heal or universal invulnerability buff. Existing damage-
                               over-time and ground hazards already present inside keep their normal behaviour.
                               Astra must stay inside the enclosure to benefit from its blocked attack paths.
Aim:                           Centred on Astra at key press, not on the mouse. Does not follow him.
Cooldown:                      90 seconds from successful worker deployment. No second enclosure while one is active.
Workers:                       Speed 200; noncombat, untargetable, cannot body-block, carry no weapons or loot.
                               They cannot be killed to interrupt construction. One shared worker sprite design.
Animation sheet(s):            sprites/astra-radio-call.png, sprites/construction-worker.png,
                               effects/deploy-beacon.png, effects/wall-horizontal.png,
                               effects/wall-vertical.png, skill-cards/card-R.png.
Sound (optional):              Radio confirmation, hammering, metal lock-in, warning beeps, harmless dismantle.

--- PLACEMENT, AI AND MULTIPLAYER RULES ---
- This kit is a design and art handoff. The attached template contains no runnable game source.
  Engine integration and multiplayer testing are still required; do not advertise this ZIP as a playable patch.
- R is an additional key beyond the source template. Add a third HUD slot and rebindable action.
  If the game already uses R for reload, move reload to another key or expose remapping; never trigger both.
- Initial balance values not supplied by the user (health, speed, damage, cooldowns, durations for E/Q,
  build time, wall size) are designer defaults. Preserve 7 police, 3 doctors, 25 HP/s, 2 workers,
  4 walls and 45-second wall lifetime unless the user explicitly changes those requirements.
- Keep the template's Q unlock of 100 non-boss kills; after unlocking, Q is cooldown-based for that match.
  Do not require another 100 kills for every activation. Show the unlock progress in the HUD.
- E and Q must find free spawn positions for ALL required NPCs before committing. If placement fails,
  reject the cast with a clear HUD message and do not consume cooldown. Do not spawn a smaller squad.
- R must fit inside the map and avoid the radio tower, permanent scenery, existing player structures,
  another active enclosure, and bosses intersecting its footprint. Search nearby candidate centres within
  120px only if Astra remains inside the proposed interior; otherwise reject without starting cooldown.
- Small zombies intersecting the interior/walls when R finishes are moved to the nearest free exterior
  positions without damage. Reserve these positions before committing. If a boss or immovable obstacle
  enters before completion, or safe displacement becomes impossible, cancel and refund R; do not crush,
  trap or teleport a boss. Release the workers and remove scaffolds on cancellation.
- Workers phase through allies and their own scaffolds. Police/doctors use obstacle-aware pathfinding,
  local separation and no allied body blocking. They must not phase through permanent map geometry.
- Doctors share a server-side patient reservation table across every Astra. Reconsider idle targets every
  0.25s and on patient state changes. Break a reservation after 2s without a viable path. Healing uses
  elapsed server time: min(maxHP, currentHP + 25 * dt). Deduplicate repeated network events.
- Police use obstacle-aware paths and line of sight, switch to threats near friendly players, and never
  fire through permanent scenery. Return toward Astra when beyond the 600px leash.
- Summon kills/damage credit the owning Astra once; boss kills do not add to Q's unlock count.
  Doctors' healing and walls built may be tracked separately. Workers are not playable teammates.
- Optional wave scaling: use the SAME friendly-summon multiplier as existing allies for police damage
  and police/doctor HP. If no such system exists, use the base values above until playtesting. Do not
  independently multiply officer count, doctor count, healing rate, wall count or wall lifetime.
- Caps PER ASTRA: 7 police, 3 doctors, 2 workers while building, 4 active wall sides. Multiple Astras may
  coexist, but enclosures cannot overlap. Test the worst case of 8 Astras; keep AI server-authoritative.
- A downed Astra's already deployed units/structure finish their normal lifetimes and can help teammates;
  police follow the nearest living teammate until Astra revives. Fully dead or disconnected owner:
  remove police/doctors, cancel pending casts/builds, but keep a COMPLETED enclosure until its original
  45-second expiry. Match end removes everything immediately. No summons carry into the next match.
- A cast reserves its own ability until completion; repeated key-down packets cannot duplicate summons.
  One radio call at a time. Interrupt before deployment if Astra becomes downed/dead; do not spend cooldown.
- Render footprint and countdown independently from sprite pixels. Wall images show modular visual segments;
  collision is the exact four rectangles specified above, never inferred from decorative overhangs.
- Preserve chunky pixels with nearest-neighbour scaling. Key out the magenta matte during sprite import.
  The supplied manifest records actual crop regions; do not assume every sheet has the same pixel dimensions.
- Shared radio animation: 0.15s raise, 0.15s hold, 0.15s lower. Reuse it for Q, E and R; three duplicate
  body sheets would add no visual difference. Only the summoned unit/effect/card changes.
