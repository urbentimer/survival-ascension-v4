SOFEA THE OPERATOR - SOURCE ART AND IMPORT NOTES

ART STATUS
Fourteen PNG files were generated with the built-in image tool, individually,
in the order requested by the original kit. Portrait identity was reused for
character sheets and occupied vehicles. All animation/effect images are on
magenta; portrait and two-card artwork are on black. Only the skill-card image
contains text. Labels are English. The original files are preserved at native
resolution, without artificial upscaling or post-generation image edits.

These are generated SOURCE sheets. They are not a validated engine atlas.
Frame counts and separated source regions were checked. Their intended directions
were visually inspected, but pixel-perfect pivots and temporal animation fidelity
remain to be checked in the game's actual renderer.

KNOWN SOURCE DEVIATIONS FROM THE TEMPLATE'S IDEAL EXPORT RULES
1. Magenta is visually consistent but is not uniformly the exact RGB #FF00FF value.
   Edge/background samples include approximately RGB (240,12,240) to (244,32,241).
2. Several sprite sheets have gaps below the requested 40px minimum. Figures do not
   intentionally overlap, but importing as a naive equally divided grid is unsafe.
3. Frame widths, feet baselines and rendered size vary slightly. Source silhouettes
   must be aligned before animation; neither per-frame bounding-box centers nor
   automatic scaling of each individual pose are reliable animation pivots.
4. Some diagonal views, especially bat and helicopter upper-right, are closer to
   side views than a strict 45-degree turn. Verify and retouch in the art pass if
   the game's direction changes expose a visible jump.
5. Rotor and track poses are source variations, not a verified seamless animation.
   Evaluate at the intended playback speed; retouch phases if they appear to wobble.

IMPORT ROUTE
- Use frame-map.json to locate source regions. Coordinates are absolute source
  pixels: [x, y, width, height]; row and column numbers start at zero. Rectangles
  use detected gaps and keep each pose/effect together. They are not engine pivots.
- Background analysis in that JSON was for finding source geometry only. Do not
  blindly apply its broad magenta threshold to erase pixels: it may damage pink
  hair or anti-aliased edges. Use edge-connected background selection, careful
  color tolerance and hair-edge review when producing keyed/transparent assets.
- After keying, place aligned poses in equal export cells with at least 40px of
  clear margin to neighboring art. Export exact #FF00FF backgrounds if retaining
  the original kit's keying pipeline, or RGBA if the game importer supports it.
- One scale per sheet/character set. Do not normalize every pose to its individual
  bounding box: strides, weapon arcs and muzzle flashes legitimately change bounds.
- Align survivor feet and pelvis across frames. Align vehicle ground origin, hull
  turret ring and turret circular base. Use these same pivots across all directions.
- Reconcile survivor scale between the different native source resolutions below.
  Set the final tank and helicopter world sizes from character.txt, not raw PNG size.
- Keep nearest-neighbor sampling and avoid bilinear enlargement. Increase gameplay
  asset resolution through a new art pass if required; interpolation adds no detail.

SUGGESTED PLAYBACK (DESIGN DEFAULTS, NOT TESTED GAME VALUES)
Weapon: hold idle column 1; walk loop columns 2,3,4,3 at 8 fps.
Rifle: column 5 aiming, brief column 6 shot flash synchronized to actual fire events.
Melee: columns 5 and 6 are wind-up/strike key poses; existing combat timing determines
the contact frame. Use existing axe charge duration; do not let art alter damage timing.
Radio: four columns over 0.8 seconds; same body action for E and Q.
Hull: four columns at 8 fps while driving, column 1 while stationary.
Turret: ready hold, flash about 0.08s, recoil about 0.10s, settle about 0.12s;
then ready until the next allowed cannon shot. This is not a continuous firing loop.
Helicopter: four rotor columns at about 12 fps, continuous while occupied.
Rocket: six propulsion phases at about 12 fps. Cannon shell: four phases at 12 fps.
Explosion: six frames over about 0.45s, once. Dust: six frames over about 0.6s, once.
Gameplay damage is authoritative and independent of how many effect frames render.

SKILL CARD CUT
The two card regions occupy left and right halves of the 1774 x 887 image.
Each half is 887 x 887 including its black padding. Left is E Steel Convoy;
right is Q Sky Reign. Do not key black out of the card artwork.

VERIFICATION BOUNDARY
Verified: file presence/readability, image dimensions, template headings, required
controls/durations, source counts (342 sprite/effect poses), and ZIP integrity.
Not verified: final keyed atlas, pivot alignment, seamless playback, damage balance,
collision, server replication, character-selection wiring or in-game visual quality.
