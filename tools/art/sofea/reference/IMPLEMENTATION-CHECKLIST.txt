SOFEA THE OPERATOR - GAME INTEGRATION ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST

All items below are requirements to run in the real game; they are NOT marked tested.
The source project was not supplied with the template.

E - TANK
[ ] E is available without Q's kill unlock; valid call boards Sofea automatically.
[ ] Blocked spawn cancels without cooldown or moving her into a wall.
[ ] Ride is 30.0 seconds from boarding; call/arrival time is not subtracted.
[ ] WASD movement works; diagonal speed is normalized; hull faces travel.
[ ] Turret follows mouse independently; click/held click fires no faster than 1.5s.
[ ] Shell impact/falloff applies once per target per shell, including direct hits.
[ ] Moving tracks damage zombies with a per-target 0.75s cooldown and swept contact.
[ ] A stationary tank causes zero run-over damage; bosses use the 10-damage rule.
[ ] Walls, tower, team barricades and solid bosses block tank travel.
[ ] No friendly fire or accidental structure damage; no health reset on boarding.
[ ] At expiry, safely exit, restore prior weapon and begin 30-second E cooldown.

Q - HELICOPTER
[ ] Locked at 99 non-boss kills; unlocked at 100; boss kills do not advance it.
[ ] Once unlocked, future uses need the cooldown only, not another 100 kills.
[ ] Call automatically boards Sofea; piloting lasts 30.0 seconds from boarding.
[ ] WASD moves; mouse aims the nose; held click fires paired salvos every 1.5s.
[ ] Rocket projectiles travel visibly to the clamped mouse point; no auto-aim.
[ ] Each rocket deals one explosion hit per target; overlapping rockets can both hit.
[ ] Ground melee misses; ranged/aerial-capable attacks use helicopter defense rules.
[ ] Fly over low obstacles and zombies, but respect tall blockers and map bounds.
[ ] At expiry, safely exit, restore prior weapon and begin 60-second Q cooldown.

SHARED CONTROL AND LIFECYCLE
[ ] Reject the other ability during radio call/ride, without consuming its cooldown.
[ ] Repeated or held E/Q cannot extend rides, create duplicates or queue another ride.
[ ] Personal weapons, dash, medkits, repairs, revives and equipment are disabled only
    while mounted; inventory and ordinary control state return on dismount.
[ ] Vehicle damage passes to current survivor health with exactly one defense multiplier.
[ ] Fatal damage leads to normal downed/revive state on safe ground, not a free revive.
[ ] Expiry inside obstacles/enemy groups uses safe exit search and fallback behavior.
[ ] Short dismount protection expires and cannot be refreshed by duplicate events.
[ ] No new shots at/after ride expiry; earlier shots finish with correct owner credit.
[ ] Wave transitions preserve ride duration and cooldown; match end clears all state.
[ ] Disconnect/despawn removes its vehicle and sound without affecting other players.
