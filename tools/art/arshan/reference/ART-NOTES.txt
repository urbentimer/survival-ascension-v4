ARSHAN THE SLASHER - ART STATUS AND IMPORT GUIDE

STYLE AND PACKAGE
The supplied NEW kit's Marcus guide/examples were inspected and used as references.
Portrait defines identity; sprite sheets use chunky three-head-tall anatomy,
large white hair/red oni mask, scarred bare torso/back, red sash and dark trousers.
All body/effect sheets are landscape 1536 x 1024. Maximum 24 frames per image.
Every 8-direction action is split into A/B files, even the four-column skills.
Each skill card is a separate square file. Only cards contain labels, in English.
Images were created with the built-in image tool. The crimson orbit was edited
with the same tool to increase its spacing; originals are otherwise unresampled.

DIRECTION ORDER
A rows: DOWN, DOWN-RIGHT, RIGHT, UP-RIGHT.
B rows: UP, UP-LEFT, LEFT, DOWN-LEFT.
Rows/columns in frame-map.json start at zero. Source rectangles are absolute
pixels [x, y, width, height]. Measured boxes help isolate each pose without
assuming perfect equal-cell placement. They are NOT tested animation pivots.

SOURCE LIMITATIONS TO RESOLVE AT IMPORT
- Generated backgrounds are visually magenta but have small RGB variations;
  sample corner values are around (240,14,241), not uniformly exact #FF00FF.
  Key/select connected background carefully, review halos, and export exact
  magenta or alpha according to the existing game's importer.
- Some body sheets have measured empty gutters below 40px (down to about26px
  horizontally and32px vertically). The corrected orbit has wider clearance.
  Repack aligned poses into uniform export cells with at least40px separation.
- Source body sizes/feet anchors vary slightly across poses. Establish the
  feet/pelvis anchor for each direction and keep it consistent; do not resize
  individual frames based on attack-arc or weapon bounding-box width.
- Inspect walk stride continuity, handedness and attack direction in playback.
  Front/rear strike poses sometimes emphasize lateral swings; engine hitboxes
  must still face the intended aim direction. Retouch any visible pose jumps.
- The body spin loop and four orbit phases are separate layers. Align their
  center/hand position and visually test together; no live compositing test
  has been performed in the game renderer.

READABILITY
Target the same on-screen standing survivor height as Marcus (about54px).
Use nearest-neighbor sampling; avoid bilinear blur. Portrait proportions must
not determine sprite size. Bright mask, white hair, thick outlines and short
legs are intended to preserve the silhouette at this scale.

WEAPON ANIMATION
Columns 1-6 are idle, walk1, walk2, walk3, wind-up and strike.
Use walk columns 2,3,4,3 as the initial loop at approximately8fps and verify
strides. Ordinary melee timing is owned by combat code, not number of frames.
Fire-axe wind-up can be held for the existing1-second charge. White arcs are
already drawn in strike frames; do not add duplicate hit effects/damage.

E BODY/PROJECTILE CHOREOGRAPHY
Column1 has the held axe; show it only before launch.
At launch switch to column2 and spawn effects/hook-axe.png once.
Use column3 during self-pull; column4 during arrival/retraction.
Draw/tile effects/chain-link.png from the active hand to the projectile's eye;
trim/overlap terminal links to avoid double caps, and use a consistent width.
Rotate the RIGHT-facing axe/chain assets to the hook's direction. These effects
do not require an eight-direction source sheet. Keep chain taut while pulling.
Play hook-impact's four frames once at contact (about0.2s); no looping sparks.
Never display an extra held axe during the launched-hook phases.

Q BODY/ORBIT CHOREOGRAPHY
Use four body columns as a subtle loop (about8fps), oriented to mouse heading.
Use four orbit phases as a clockwise loop (starting about16fps), independent
of body heading. Move the common pivot with Arshan. Visible red heat is
cosmetic; it must not apply unintended burn stacks.
Scale the effect to match the specified260px gameplay radius. The source
ellipse represents perspective: do not infer damage radius from its height.
The included orbit contains the red axe and a short chain/haft stub. If the
hand-to-eye gap remains visible after compositing, complete it with the chain
segment effect, matched to the current orbit phase. Do not draw a second axe.
Draw the orbit's back part behind Arshan and front part in front, respecting
the game's world occlusion. Use an existing renderer mask/clip where needed.
Wind-up and cooldown are gameplay states, not extra damaging frames.

VERIFICATION BOUNDARY
Checked: all files readable, dimensions, <=24 frames per sheet, A/B pair count,
272 separated source poses, required appearances, card text, character-sheet
headings, requested controls and ZIP integrity.
Not tested: final color-key/alpha exports, seamless playback, engine pivots,
actual hook collision, damage balance, ultimate occlusion or multiplayer state.
