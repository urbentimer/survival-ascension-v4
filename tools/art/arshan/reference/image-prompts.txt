Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE full-body detailed character-select portrait for Survival Ascension. Bare muscular torso, shoulders and arms covered in numerous HEALED pale scars including a diagonal chest scar and short crossing scars, no blood, no wounds, no tattoos. It is a chain-axe, not a scythe, not two axes, not a long polearm. Holds chain in one hand with a readable length hanging in a loose arc, axe head resting beside him; rest of chain coiled in other hand. Silhouette intimidating, calm poised menace. Gritty polished detailed PIXEL ART with clean thick outlines and bright readable white/red accents, no blurry rendering. Portrait may have heroic anatomy; later sprites will use chibi proportions. Full figure and full axe visible, centered with generous margins, plain solid BLACK background. No text, logos, Japanese or Chinese lettering, symbols, panels or scene. Only this character.
