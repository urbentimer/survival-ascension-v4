HOW TO PLAY · FIELD MANUAL

Objective: Protect the radio tower. If its health reaches zero, the team loses. Survive 50 waves and defeat the final boss on Wave 50.

Waves: Standard waves last 90 seconds. Every fourth wave is a boss encounter; Wave 50 is the final boss. Boss waves continue until the boss is defeated.

Combat: Move with WASD or arrow keys. Your survivor automatically fires at the nearest zombie in range. Press Space to dash.

Teamwork: Press E near a downed teammate to revive them. Keep an eye on the tower and generator status.

Build & maintain: Use field-action buttons to repair the tower, repair/refuel the generator and build barricades, turrets, mines, electric fences and flame traps. Turrets, fences and flame traps need generator power.

Day & night: Night reduces visibility and increases zombie aggression. Check the HUD for current conditions.

Scoreboard: Press TAB to show or hide the live kill leaderboard. The current kill leader wears a golden crown.
