HOW TO PLAY · FIELD MANUAL

Objective: Protect the radio tower. If its health reaches zero, the team loses. Survive 50 waves and defeat the final boss on Wave 50.

Waves: Standard waves last 90 seconds, then a 10-second break before the next wave starts by itself (the host can start it sooner). Every fourth wave is a boss encounter; Wave 50 is the final boss. Boss waves continue until the boss is defeated.

Combat: Move with WASD or arrow keys. Aim with the mouse and hold the left button to shoot. Your survivor faces the way you walk. Each weapon has its own range: shotguns hit up to 3 close zombies, launchers damage an area, snipers reach furthest. Upgrades carry over when you switch weapons. Press Space to dash.

Teamwork: Press E near a downed teammate and stay close for 5 seconds to revive them. If nobody reaches them before the bleed-out timer ends (shorter each time you go down in a wave), they sit out until the next wave. Keep an eye on the tower and generator status.

Build & maintain: Use field-action buttons to repair the tower, repair/refuel the generator and build barricades, turrets, mines, electric fences and flame traps. Turrets, fences and flame traps need generator power.

Day & night: Night reduces visibility and increases zombie aggression. Check the HUD for current conditions.

Level up: A panel appears beside the map. Press 1–5 or click to pick; the fight keeps going while you choose.

Scoreboard: Press TAB to show or hide the live kill leaderboard. The current kill leader wears a golden crown.
